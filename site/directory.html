4695 Chabot Drive, Suite 200, Pleasanton, CA 94588
925-803-0663
fromthehearthomecare.com

Pricing, payment, quality, and what to ask
Levels of care

Nonmedical personal care, bathing, medication reminders, housekeeping, errands, transportation, respite, dementia support.

Pricing

Rates from March 2026: $68/hr up to one hour, $49/hr for two to three, $39/hr for four or more. Twelve-hour awake shift $395, sleeping $350. No minimums.

Payment options

Private pay. The quoted rate includes the agency commission, so there is no separate client fee. Long-term care insurance may apply; Medicare does not.

Languages and access

Office weekdays 8:30 to 4:00, with a 24-hour on-call line. Ask about language matching and dementia training.

Quality indicators

A domestic referral agency, not a licensed Home Care Organization, so caregivers are independent contractors and there is no HCO licence to verify. Background checks and caregiver liability insurance required. Verify caregivers on the California Home Care Aide Registry.

Ask them

Pricing, payment, quality, and what to ask
Levels of care

PACE integrated medical care; adult day center; rehabilitation; home support; transportation; memory-support space

Pricing

For people with both Medicare and Medi-Cal, approved PACE services generally have no deductible or copay. Medicare-only participants may owe a monthly PACE premium and Part D premium. Confirm individual eligibility and costs.

Payment options

Medicare and Medi-Cal capitation; private-pay participation may be possible. Eligibility generally includes age 55+, service-area residence, nursing-home level of care, and ability to live safely in the community.

Languages and access

Ask for current language capacity and interpretation. A listed physician profile notes English, Filipino, medical Spanish, and basic Chinese.

Quality indicators

PACE is federally and state regulated. Ask for enrollment/disenrollment rights, grievance data, network access, after-hours response, and participant satisfaction.

Ask them

Which doctors and hospitals are in network? What happens to existing specialists? How fast can home care, transportation, and urgent care be arranged?
